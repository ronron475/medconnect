# Appendix E: User’s Manual
**MedConnect: Community Healthcare and Teleconsultation Platform for Bago City**
*City Health Office (CHO) · Bago City, Negros Occidental*

# Patient Guide

## 1. System Access and Sign – In

### 1.1. Logging In
1) Open the **MedConnect** Web Application in your browser (e.g., `http://localhost/medconnect/` or your official web address).
2) On the landing page, click the **Sign In** button or the **Book Consultation** button to open the Sign-In panel.
3) Enter your registered **Email Address** in the designated field.
4) Enter your **Password**.
5) Click the **Sign In** button to open the Patient Portal.

> **Note:** If you do not have an account, click **Create Patient Account** or **Register Now** and complete the registration form. If you forget your password, use the **Forgot Password?** link available on the sign-in panel.

### 1.2. Registering a Patient Account
1) On the sign-in panel, click **Create Patient Account** to open the 3-step registration form.
2) **Step 1 (Email Verification):** Enter your active email address and click **Send Verification Code**. Check your inbox, input the 6-digit one-time PIN (OTP), and proceed.
3) **Step 2 (Identity & Residency Verification):**
   - Select your valid document type (PhilSys National ID, ePhilID, or Barangay Certificate).
   - Upload a clear photo or scanned copy of your ID. The system’s built-in Optical Character Recognition (OCR) verifies that you are an official resident of Bago City.
   - Confirm that the extracted details (Full Name, Date of Birth, and Barangay) match your official credentials.
4) **Step 3 (Patient Details & Security):**
   - Enter your contact number, age, gender, blood type, PhilHealth status, and emergency contact information.
   - Set a secure password (minimum 8 characters) and confirm it.
5) Submit the registration form and follow the on-screen prompts to access your verified patient account.

> **Note:** Provide accurate and truthful information. MedConnect is integrated directly with the City Health Office of Bago City, and your residency information ensures eligibility for municipal health services.

---

## 2. Patient Dashboard Overview

After successful sign-in, you will be directed to the **Patient Dashboard**, where you can manage your consultations, symptom assessments, and medical records.

### 2.1. Patient Portal Menu
• **Dashboard:** Returns to the main patient page displaying upcoming appointments, active session alerts, and health summaries.  
• **AI Triage / Symptoms:** Opens the symptom assessment interview to check current health concerns and obtain preliminary guidance.  
• **Consultations:** Displays your scheduled, in-progress, and past teleconsultation appointments.  
• **Messages:** Opens secure, in-app messaging with your assigned City Health Office healthcare provider.  
• **My Health / Records:** Displays issued electronic prescriptions (e-prescriptions), doctor clinical notes, and health summaries.  
• **Settings:** Allows you to review or update available profile details, contact numbers, address, and password.  
• **Logout:** Ends your current MedConnect session securely.

### 2.2. Patient Health & Appointment Summary
• **Active Teleconsultation Banner:** Displays an urgent notification with a **Join Call** button on the day of your appointment once the doctor opens the virtual room.  
• **Upcoming Consultations:** Shows the date, time slot, assigned doctor, and consultation status for upcoming sessions.  
• **Recent Triage Status:** Displays the urgency classification (Routine, Priority, or Emergency) of your latest symptom evaluation.  
• **Prescriptions & Clinical Files:** Shows the total count of active prescriptions issued by attending physicians.  
• **Care Guidance & Recommendations:** Shows preliminary care tips and home remedies based on your recorded symptoms.

---

## 3. AI Symptom Triage Assessment

### 3.1. Submitting Symptoms (English & Hiligaynon)
1) On the Patient Dashboard, locate the **AI Symptom Assessment** card or click **Check Symptoms**.
2) Type your chief complaint or describe how you are feeling in the input field.
3) You may express your symptoms in **English**, **Tagalog**, or natural **Hiligaynon** (e.g., *"May hilanat kag ubo ako sing duha na ka adlaw kag masakit akon ulo"*).
4) Answer the guided follow-up questions regarding the duration, severity, and associated symptoms.
5) Click **Submit Symptoms** to process the triage evaluation.

### 3.2. Understanding Triage Urgency Levels
• **Routine (Level 5):** Mild, non-urgent symptoms. Home care guidance is provided. You may book a routine teleconsultation slot if desired.  
• **Priority / Urgent (Level 3–4):** Moderate symptoms requiring clinical attention. The system prompts you to schedule a consultation with a City Health Office physician.  
• **Emergency (Level 1–2):** Critical symptoms detected (e.g., severe chest pain, breathing difficulties, acute trauma).

> **Note:** If an **Emergency** classification is triggered, an urgent alert banner will display Bago City Emergency Hotlines. You are instructed to proceed immediately to the **Bago City District Hospital Emergency Room** or contact your nearest Barangay Health Station.

---

## 4. Booking a Teleconsultation Appointment

### 4.1. Selecting a Doctor and Time Slot
1) From the dashboard or triage results screen, click **Book Consultation**.
2) Select an available City Health Office healthcare provider or choose General Consultation.
3) Choose an available consultation date from the schedule calendar.
4) Select an open **30-minute time slot** generated directly from the doctor’s official clinic hours.
5) Review your booking details and click **Confirm Appointment**.

### 4.2. Managing Bookings and Waitlist
• If all regular slots for your desired date are fully booked, click **Join Waitlist** to queue for openings resulting from cancellations.  
• When a slot becomes available, the system sends an automatic notification to your portal and registered email.  
• To reschedule or cancel an existing upcoming appointment, open the **Consultations** tab, select the appointment, and choose **Cancel** or **Reschedule** at least 2 hours prior to the slot.

---

## 5. Attending the Video Call Consultation

### 5.1. Joining the Virtual Consultation Room
1) On the day of your appointment, log in to MedConnect 5 to 10 minutes before your scheduled slot.
2) On your dashboard, look for the active session notification card.
3) When the doctor opens the consultation, click the **Join Call** button.
4) When prompted by your browser, click **Allow** to grant access to your **Camera** and **Microphone**.
5) You will be connected directly to the secure WebRTC video room with your attending doctor.

### 5.2. In-Call Controls and Session Extension
• **Microphone Button:** Mute or unmute your audio during the consultation.  
• **Camera Button:** Turn your video camera on or off.  
• **In-Call Chat:** Send text messages or clarify spellings if audio is interrupted.  
• **Countdown Timer:** Displays the remaining minutes of your scheduled slot (typically 30 minutes).  
• **Session Extension:** If additional time is required, the doctor may extend the session by 15 minutes. The timer will automatically update on your screen.  
• **Leave Call:** If your connection drops, click **Leave Call** and click **Join Call** again from your dashboard to reconnect.

> **Note:** Only the physician has the authorization to formally **End Consultation**. Do not close the window until the doctor confirms that your assessment, advice, and prescriptions have been finalized.

---

## 6. Accessing Medical Records & E-Prescriptions

### 6.1. Viewing Completed Consultation Summaries
1) Open the **My Health** or **Consultations** menu from the portal navigation.
2) Select the **History / Completed** tab.
3) Click on a specific consultation to view the doctor’s findings, assessment, and recommendations.

### 6.2. Viewing and Using Digital Prescriptions
1) Open **My Health** and click on the **Prescriptions** section.
2) Review the prescribed medications, including generic names, dosage, frequency, and instructions.
3) Click **View / Download E-Prescription** to open or print the digital prescription slip.
4) Present this digital prescription or printed copy to the Bago City Health Office Pharmacy or accredited community pharmacies to obtain your medicine.

---

## 7. Managing Patient Account Settings

### 7.1. Updating Profile and Contact Details
1) Click **Settings** from the Patient Portal menu.
2) Review your personal details, mobile phone number, and emergency contact details.
3) Update any fields that require revision (e.g., changed mobile number, new emergency contact person).
4) If your residential address within Bago City changes, select your new Barangay and update your Purok or street details.
5) Click **Save Changes** to store your updated record.

### 7.2. Account Security & Logout
• **Password Management:** To change your password, enter your current password, type your new password, and click **Update Password**.  
• **Privacy Protection:** Never share your login credentials with others. Your account contains confidential personal and medical data protected under the Data Privacy Act of 2012.  
• **Logging Out:** Click **Logout** at the top-right corner of the screen whenever you finish your session, especially when using a shared family computer or mobile phone.

---

## 8. Troubleshooting & Helpful Reminders

• **Cannot Log In:** Verify that your email address and password are typed correctly without extra spaces. Use the **Forgot Password?** link on the sign-in panel to recover your account.  
• **Camera or Microphone Not Working:** Click the lock or settings icon beside the website address in your browser bar. Verify that permissions for **Camera** and **Microphone** are set to **Allow**, then refresh the page.  
• **Video Call Disconnected:** Refresh the web page or return to the Patient Dashboard and click **Join Call** again. Your session remains open until ended by the physician.  
• **PhilSys ID Upload Fails:** Make sure your ID image is clear, well-lit, and not blurred. Ensure the text clearly states your address in Bago City, Negros Occidental.  
• **Prescription Not Visible:** E-prescriptions appear immediately after the doctor completes and submits the consultation record. If missing after a completed session, contact the City Health Office.  
• **Medical Emergencies:** MedConnect is intended for non-emergent outpatient teleconsultations. In cases of severe trauma, loss of consciousness, or acute chest pain, immediately proceed to the nearest emergency room.

# Healthcare Provider (Doctor) Guide

## 1. System Access and Sign – In
1) Open the MedConnect web application in your browser.
2) Click **Sign In** and enter your registered medical provider credentials.
3) Click **Sign In** to open the Provider Clinical Dashboard.

> **Note:** Healthcare provider accounts require administrative verification of your PRC Medical License and City Health Office accreditation before login access is activated.

---

## 2. Schedule and Slot Management
1) Navigate to **Schedule** from the provider navigation menu.
2) Define your weekly consultation hours by specifying the start and end times for each active weekday (e.g., Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 12:00 PM and 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM).
3) Set your standard **Slot Duration** (default: 30 minutes).
4) Click **Save Schedule & Generate Slots**. The system automatically generates bookable appointment slots across the next 4 weeks.

---

## 3. Consultation Queue and Workstation
1) Open the **Consultation Queue** to review scheduled patients sorted by appointment time and AI triage priority.
2) **Session Access Rule:** You can only click **Open Session** for consultations scheduled for **today** or appointments currently marked as `in_consultation`. Future appointments are protected to avoid inadvertent early starts.
3) In the clinical workstation:
   - **Video Room:** Click **Start Video Consultation** to initiate the WebRTC video stream.
   - **AI Transcript Assistant:** View real-time speech-to-text transcriptions with automatic Hiligaynon-to-English medical keyword translations (e.g., *sakit dughan* → *chest pain*). Extracted symptoms and medications are highlighted.
   - Click **Insert into SOAP** to paste the generated AI summary directly into the Assessment field.
   - **SOAP Documentation:** Complete the Subjective, Objective, Assessment, and Plan fields.
   - **Session Extension (+15 min):** Click **+15 min** if the consultation requires additional time. The system verifies that no conflicting patient is booked in the immediate next slot before extending.
   - **E-Prescription & Referral:** Issue prescriptions with generic names, dosages, and instructions, or generate an electronic referral to Bago City District Hospital.
   - **Ending Consultation:** Click **End Consultation** to close the video session, generate the patient’s records, and clear the queue.

# Barangay Health Worker (BHW) Guide

## 1. System Access and Sign – In

### 1.1. Logging In
1) Open the **MedConnect** web application in your browser.
2) On the sign-in panel, enter your registered BHW **Email Address**.
3) Enter your **Password**.
4) Click the **Sign In** button to open the BHW Portal.

> **Note:** BHW accounts are verified and registered by the City Health Office System Administrator and linked directly to an authorized Barangay assignment (e.g., *BHW - Brgy. Poblacion*).

### 1.2. Sector Verification & Session Indicators
• **Barangay Sector Display:** The bottom-left user card displays your full role title and assigned sector (e.g., `Barangay Health Worker` / `BHW - Brgy. Poblacion`). All patient records and operations are restricted to your assigned barangay to protect citizen privacy.  
• **Header Status:** The top navigation bar displays the live system Date and Time (`October 13, 2026 | 8:53 PM`), a **Theme** toggle (Light/Dark mode), and a Notification Bell with an unread badge counter.

---

## 2. BHW Dashboard Overview

After signing in, you are presented with the **BHW Dashboard**, providing real-time operational analytics and security alerts for your barangay.

### 2.1. Portal Layout and Navigation Menu
• **Header Bar:**
  - **Sidebar Toggle (☰):** Collapses or expands the navigation sidebar for easier viewing on smaller laptops or tablets.
  - **Page Title:** Displays the active module name (**Dashboard**).
  - **Date & Time:** Real-time clock for timestamping community activities.
  - **Theme Switcher:** Toggles between standard light mode and high-contrast dark theme.
  - **Notification Bell:** Alerts you to system events, patient registration updates, and security notices with an unread counter.
• **Left Sidebar Menu:**
  - **Dashboard:** Returns to the main activity overview and notification stream.
  - **BARANGAY OPERATIONS:**
    - **Patient List:** Directory of all registered residents in your barangay with search, filter, and export options.
    - **Patient Registration:** Assisted registration form to enroll walk-in community residents.
    - **Records:** Patient health summaries, medical history, and prescribed medications repository.
    - **Appointment History:** Real-time queue and historical log of consultation appointments and follow-up schedules.
  - **REPORTS:**
    - **My Activity Log:** An immutable audit record of actions taken by your account.
  - **ACCOUNT:**
    - **Profile:** Manage your contact number, display name, and profile picture.
  - **Logout:** Ends your current BHW session.

### 2.2. Activity Trends & Consultation Statistics
• **Activity Panel:** Displays live consultation trends among residents in your assigned barangay.  
• **Time Range Filter:** Use the period dropdown button (**Week**, **Days**, or **Months**) to adjust the chart display.  
• **Consultation Trends Chart:** A bar/line chart that tracks daily consultation volumes across the week (Wednesday to Tuesday), allowing you to identify community health surges.

### 2.3. Recent Notifications & Platform Activity
• **Live Notification Stream:** Displays real-time platform notices with color-coded severity badges:
  - ⚠️ **Warning (e.g., New Device Login):** Alerts you when your account is accessed from an unrecognized browser or IP address. Click the notification to review connected devices.
  - ℹ️ **Information (e.g., Login Successful):** Logs valid sign-ins from recognized devices.
• **View All Button:** Directs you to the full notifications center to view and clear unread alerts.

---

## 3. Barangay Operations: Patient Management

### 3.1. Assisted Patient Registration
1) In the left sidebar under **BARANGAY OPERATIONS**, click **Patient Registration**.
2) Fill out the resident's personal details: Full Name, Birthdate, Gender, Age, and Contact Number.
3) Specify their residential Purok or Street within your assigned Barangay.
4) Encode their PhilSys Card Number / ePhilID and upload proof of residency if available.
5) Enter PhilHealth ID number, blood type, and emergency contact information.
6) Submit the registration. The resident is immediately enrolled into the Bago City CHO digital database and linked to your barangay.

> **Note:** Use this module to assist seniors, indigent families, or residents without smartphones or internet access during field rounds or barangay health center duty.

### 3.2. Searching and Managing the Patient List
1) Click **Patient List** from the sidebar.
2) Use the search bar to locate residents by **Name**, **Patient ID**, or **Contact Number**.
3) Review the resident summary table showing demographic data and assigned risk flags.
4) Click on a patient record to open the action drawer to view details or update contact and medical data.
5) Use **Export CSV** or **Print / Export PDF** to generate official masterlists for barangay health station documentation.

---

## 4. Barangay Operations: Records and Appointments

### 4.1. Accessing Resident Health Records
1) Click **Records** under **BARANGAY OPERATIONS**.
2) Search for a resident using the search bar.
3) Click **Open Record** to view the patient modal containing three tabs:
   - **Profile Tab:** Demographic and contact information.
   - **Medical History Tab:** Past diagnoses, allergy notes, and consultation findings.
   - **Prescriptions Tab:** Electronic prescriptions issued by City Health Office physicians for that resident.
4) Verify prescription details to assist residents in claiming medications from the Bago City Health Office or local distribution drives.

### 4.2. Appointment History & Follow-Up Tracking
1) Click **Appointment History** from the sidebar.
2) View the live queue of scheduled, completed, and pending appointments for residents in your barangay.
3) Use the top tabs to switch between:
   - **All:** Full appointment and follow-up history.
   - **Appointments:** Teleconsultation bookings with CHO doctors.
   - **Follow-Ups:** Prescribed follow-up dates requiring community monitoring.
4) Use the live status column to remind residents of upcoming virtual or face-to-face follow-up consultations.

---

## 5. Reports: Personal Audit Trail

### 5.1. Viewing My Activity Log
1) In the left sidebar under **REPORTS**, click **My Activity Log**.
2) Review the chronological, tamper-proof record of all actions performed under your account (e.g., patient registration, record viewing, updates, and logins).
3) Entries display the exact **Timestamp**, **Action Taken**, **Module**, and **Target Patient**.

### 5.2. Filtering & Exporting Logs
1) Use the **Period** filter to narrow entries (Today, This Week, This Month, or Custom Date Range).
2) Filter by specific **Module** or search for particular keywords using the search box.
3) Click **CSV** to download a spreadsheet copy or **Print** to generate a hard copy for administrative submission.

---

## 6. Account Management & Security

### 6.1. Managing Profile Information
1) Click **Profile** under the **ACCOUNT** section of the sidebar.
2) Review your personal details, email, and assigned barangay.
3) Upload an official ID photo or profile picture to display in the portal header.
4) Update your active contact phone number so health office staff and physicians can reach you.
5) Click **Save Changes**.

### 6.2. Theme Customization & Session Security
• **Theme Toggle:** Click the **Theme** button in the top navigation bar to toggle between Light and Dark interface themes for optimal readability in varying lighting conditions.  
• **Device Security Alerts:** If you receive a **New Device Login** notification that was not initiated by you, immediately report the event to the City Health Office IT administrator and update your password.

### 6.3. Logging Out
1) Locate the **Logout** button at the bottom of the left sidebar.
2) Click **Logout** to end your active session.
3) Always close the browser when operating shared health center computers or mobile devices.

---

## 7. Troubleshooting & Helpful Reminders

• **Patient from Another Barangay Appears:** Your account is scoped to your assigned barangay only. If a patient moved to your sector, assist them in updating their address in their profile or contact the CHO Admin to reassign their record.  
• **Notifications Not Updating:** Check your internet connection. Click the **Refresh** button or reload the page to fetch the latest platform updates.  
• **Unrecognized Login Alert:** If you see a warning badge for a new device login, verify whether you recently signed in from a different smartphone or workstation.  
• **Data Privacy Compliance:** All resident records accessed through the BHW portal are confidential under the Data Privacy Act of 2012. Never disclose resident medical information to unauthorized individuals.
